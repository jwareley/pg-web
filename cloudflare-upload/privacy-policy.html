PatchGrid iPad App — App-Specific Privacy Statement

The PatchGrid iPad app processes MIDI, patch data, device names, and PatchGrid data on the iPad and through the connected Korg M3. The app does not transmit that information to Stringline Productions or to third parties.

The PatchGrid iPad app does not collect personal information, account information, payment information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. It does not provide cloud patch storage, cloud synchronisation, or in-app licence registration. PatchGrid files created or imported in the app remain in the app or on the iPad unless you explicitly manage or export them using iPad file-management features.

The website, purchases, support services, and third-party providers described elsewhere in this policy are separate from the PatchGrid iPad app and are not integrated into the app. This app-specific statement applies to the iPad version of PatchGrid.

Local files can be removed or managed using the app and iPad file-management features. Questions about this app-specific statement may be sent to privacy@virtualcontrollers.co.za.
