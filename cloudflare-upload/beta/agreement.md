## 1. Purpose

SLP grants the Beta Tester temporary access to the pre-release PatchGrid – M3 Edition application (“Application”) for testing, evaluation, and feedback.

The beta programme has been tailored to specific users based on their Korg M3 configuration, iPad device, operating system, and connected peripherals. The Application may not function correctly in every configuration.

## 2. Beta stages

The beta programme will consist of the stages SLP determines are necessary to complete the beta-testing process, but will not exceed three stages.

Each stage is expected to last one month. SLP may adjust the timing, duration, requirements, or intervals between stages to allow for necessary fixes and updates.

“Required Stages” means all stages in which the Beta Tester is required by SLP to participate. The applicable stages, testing tasks, minimum requirements, and deadlines will be provided through the PG Beta Portal or in written instructions from SLP.

## 3. Testing commitment

The Beta Tester agrees to perform the required tests during the beta-testing period and for the time indicated in their beta-programme application.

The Beta Tester must:

- complete assigned tests and activities;
- submit test results by the required deadlines;
- provide accurate, useful, and reasonably detailed feedback;
- report faults and compatibility issues;
- respond to reasonable beta-programme communications; and
- follow SLP’s testing instructions and programme requirements.

## 4. Individual stage requirements

Requirements may differ between Beta Testers based on their equipment, configuration, experience, and testing role.

The applicable requirements for each Beta Tester will be managed dynamically through the PG Beta Portal or provided in writing by SLP. These requirements form part of the Beta Tester’s testing obligations, even though they are not reproduced in this Agreement.

SLP may update the requirements where reasonably necessary to reflect testing progress, software changes, or newly identified issues. Updated requirements will be communicated through the PG Beta Portal or in writing.

## 5. Productive Participation

“Productive Participation” means that the Beta Tester complies with the minimum requirements for the beta stage in which they are enrolled.

SLP will assess Productive Participation reasonably, taking into account completed activities, feedback quality, responsiveness, testing progress, and compliance with the applicable stage requirements.

## 6. Removal from the programme

SLP may remove the Beta Tester from the beta programme if SLP reasonably determines that the Beta Tester:

- is not completing the required activities;
- is not participating productively;
- fails to comply with this Agreement or programme requirements;
- shares confidential information, login details, or unauthorised Application copies; or
- otherwise undermines the beta programme.

Removal may result in immediate revocation of access and loss of eligibility for the final Application promotional code.

## 7. Voluntary withdrawal

The Beta Tester may withdraw from the beta programme at any time without requesting SLP’s permission.

A Beta Tester is encouraged to provide SLP with a courtesy message explaining the withdrawal and, where reasonably possible, the reason for leaving. Failure to provide such a message will not prevent withdrawal.

A Beta Tester who withdraws before completing all Required Stages will generally not qualify for the final PatchGrid – M3 Edition promotional code.

However, where the Beta Tester withdraws for a reasonable reason and SLP determines that their participation up to the withdrawal date was meaningful, SLP may, at its discretion, provide a partial award or other recognition. Such an award is not guaranteed and will be determined case by case.
