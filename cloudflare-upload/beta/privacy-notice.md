# PatchGrid – M3 Edition Beta Programme Privacy Notice

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 1 September 2026

Stringline Productions, trading as Stringline Productions and operating the Virtual Controllers brand, collects beta-programme information to administer tester access, assign stage requirements, record test results, measure progress and time, investigate issues, and determine eligibility for the final application promotional code.

We collect the tester’s account identifier, full name, email address, public/chosen display name, application and equipment details, operating-system and peripheral details, testing availability, agreement decisions, checkbox selections, timestamps, stage progress, responses, issue reports, withdrawal/removal/completion status, and promotional-code availability records.

Cloudflare services, including Workers and D1, may process and store this information. Access is restricted to authorised Stringline Productions administrators and service providers required to operate the portal. We do not require handwritten signatures or uploaded signatures.

Records are retained for no longer than six months from 1 September 2026, except where a longer period is required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. You may contact SLP about your information, corrections, withdrawal, or privacy questions at privacy@virtualcontrollers.co.za.

By selecting the required privacy confirmation in the portal, you confirm that you have read this notice and understand this processing. This notice is version-controlled; a later version does not overwrite a notice previously accepted.
